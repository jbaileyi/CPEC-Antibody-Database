# Changelog

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-11)
- Antibody Tracker tab: stage from candidate to passed, order and receipt dates, lot, price, titration log with staining index and best dilution.
- CSV import of products (Find Antibodies tab).
- More facts per marker from UniProt: other names, length, mass, membrane-spanning segment count, solved structures.

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-11)
- Fixed: My Records no longer lists literature reports as your tests. Own tests and literature reports are separate tables, and pass counts are split.
- 29 more markers (keep, remove and epithelial roles) and 22 clone names, all labeled general knowledge until checked.
- New tabs: Panel Designer (channel clashes, secondaries, azide, immunogen checks), Experiments (sort purity, yield, viability), Overview (coverage, registry counts per marker, gaps to fill).

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-11)
- Schema, 18 seed markers, 14 antibody records (13 seed_unverified), 2 validation records, 8 references.
- Automatic UniProt topology and marker facts, Protein Atlas choroid plexus RNA, Europe PMC papers.
- Vendor lookup through the Antibody Registry with datasheet checks, order list, staining plans.
- Ranked test list, sort strategy, two-click result logging, data downloads.
- All markers are listed and ranked (no cap). Cell-description question removed. Evidence labels replace the default 'not yet'. Marker discovery from the Human Protein Atlas.
